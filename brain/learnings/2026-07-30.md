# Learnings — 2026-07-30

## Facts-only subagent briefs beat context-loaded ones for external-repo study

Four repo deep-dives ran as read-only subagents given ZERO factory context —
just hardened security rules (content-as-data, never-execute) and a 7-section
factual brief. Separating fact-gathering from fit-assessment kept the facts
reusable across two independent recommendation passes and made injection
defense trivial (the agent has nothing to be steered toward). Encoded into
`/repo-scout`'s embedded brief.

## The grammar was the method all along

`references/README.md`'s entry shape (what-it-is → what-to-mine-with-paths →
verdict → adoption gate → watch trigger) turned out to be a complete assessment
protocol — the 2026-07-30 four-repo cycle just executed it deliberately for the
first time. When a prose convention survives three generations of entries
(gbrain era → metaharness era → this), it's a process wanting to be a skill.
Now it is one.

## A 7k-star repo shipped the control group for our own open want

"Skill creation from experience" sat unbuilt on the mine list since the
hermes-agent study. OpenSpace ran that experiment without brakes and committed
the output: 203 auto-generated skills, ~53% harness-workarounds, mutation-chain
names, no dedup/retire step. Surprise-grade evidence that the MECE gate +
curator + eval ratchet are load-bearing, not ceremony — and a free spec for the
brakes auto-capture must ship with (`brain/anti-patterns/auto-captured-skill-landfill.md`).

## Marketing-vs-code gaps clustered on the same three tells

All four repos: headline numbers with the producing harness absent or results
uncommitted; a flagship feature inert at stock settings; safety language ahead
of safety code. The tells are cheap to check (find the bench harness, read the
default config, grep the safety gate) — now steps 3/5/6 of the scout brief.
Corollary for our own copy: the honest-copy principle is a differentiator, not
a handicap.

## Static delimiters are a judge vulnerability; minted ids are free

Our eval judge wrapped SUT output in fixed `<<<OUTPUT` fences — forgeable by
any output that includes the closing fence. Adrian's per-call UUID boundary
costs one `crypto.randomUUID()` and closes the class. The boundary-test caught
a real subtlety immediately: mentioning the literal open tag in the judge's own
instructions duplicates the tag string — describe the region, never repeat the
delimiter.
