# 2026-08-29

- **An architecture audit against an external framework can suggest a component
  Hamzaish already has, built differently.** Scoring `brain/` against Slite's
  Company Brain research (4-component framework: signals / remembering /
  dreaming-pruning / speaking-searching) proposed a Sylph-style `_drafts/` review
  queue for brain edits. It wasn't a real gap — `/learn-loop`'s propose → ratify →
  promote is already that gate for anything that becomes a guardrail, and ad-hoc
  edits happen with the operator watching in-session. Check what exists before
  building the profiled shape. → `brain/decision-log/2026-08-29-company-brain-phase-1.md`
- **A planned check can fire on a permanent constant instead of a signal — verify
  against real data before building it.** The plan called for a telemetry-based
  drift check (status claims vs. Stripe/PostHog/Sentry connector state). Querying
  every `product.config.json` first showed zero of 23 products have ever wired an
  analytics ID — "blind telemetry" is a constant, not something that drifts.
  Swapped for a git-log-based status-staleness check instead, which can't
  false-positive on a missing key the same way.
- **A staleness check needs verdict-awareness or it's mostly noise.** A naive
  21-day-since-last-status-update threshold flagged 61% of the portfolio; adding
  the `AUTOPILOT`-verdict exclusion (products the portfolio already decided not to
  touch) cut that to 7 genuinely worth a look. `candidate_guardrail:` — a
  "dreaming & pruning" check that ignores its own portfolio's deliberate-pause
  decisions will train the operator to ignore the check.
