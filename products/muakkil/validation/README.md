# Validation Ledger — Muakkil (موكّل)

The momentum-first validation rail, made checkable. `scripts/check-validation.ts muakkil` reads the **State** line below.

**The rail**: before expensive or irreversible bets, aim for ~5 conversations with target-profile users. Cheap, fast, reversible builds are their own validation — the ship is the test.
**The escape hatch**: building first is allowed (momentum is the default) — but it must be *recorded as debt here*, never happen silently.

## The hypothesis under test

> A non-technical founder who built something on an AI builder (Lovable, Bolt, v0, Base44) and
> then stalled — no users, no launch, no distribution — feels that stall as a real, nameable
> problem, and will pay someone to run the launch half for them.

**Target profile**: shipped or nearly-shipped something on an AI builder in the last ~12 months,
non-technical or lightly technical, and it did not reach meaningful users. *Not* target profile:
engineers who could do the distribution themselves and chose not to.

## Gate — due 2026-08-16

Pass requires **both**:
1. **≥3 of 5** raise the post-build stall **unprompted** — before Muakkil is described to them.
2. **≥1** names a figure they would pay to have the launch half handled.

Clause 2 was added 2026-08-13. The original gate tested only whether the pain is real; it never
tested willingness to pay, while the traction gate on 2026-10-17 requires a paying concierge
client. Opinions are free.

**Honesty control**: "unprompted" is only claimable if the **question that preceded it** is
logged. If Muakkil was described first, the mention does not count toward clause 1.

**Recruiting hook**: Lovable's $400M raise at $13.3B is the opener — "you built something on
Lovable, what happened after?" recruits from exactly the churned population that is the market.

**On fail**: the wedge is wrong. Do not build import-and-launch. Re-open the pivot decision.
