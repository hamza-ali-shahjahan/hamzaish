# GetHired — Scope

## Does

- A public leaderboard per function (12 of them) for **both** candidates and companies.
- Every profile opens at **$1**. You take a position by paying strictly more than the holder.
- Holds the spot until somebody pays more — no rental period, no expiry — and shows the
  clock that proves it ("#1 IN AI · HELD 3D 7H").
- Real money: Stripe Checkout, settled by a verified webhook, auto-refunded if the spot is
  taken while you are paying.
- A #1 company surfaces up to five openings, each stacked with matching candidates ordered
  by what they are bidding.
- Onboarding from a pasted profile URL, AI-assisted where a key exists and heuristic where
  it does not.
- Works on a phone. That is a product requirement, not a nice-to-have.

## Deliberately does NOT

- No priority credits, subscriptions, application fees, or per-post charges. The platform
  takes a percentage of winning bids; that is the whole revenue model.
- No invented "Top Applicant" score. Matching is legible and disclosed, and among eligible
  candidates the ranking is money.
- No email notifications. The only mail it sends is a sign-in link.
- No scraping. Profiles exist because someone submitted them.
- No passwords.
