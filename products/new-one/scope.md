# New One — Scope

Bar to add a feature: it must make the **argument** clearer or more testable. This is a
proposal, not a product — anything that makes it look more like an operating company than
it is, is out of scope by definition.

## Does
- States the structure in full: Commons, Ventures, Members, and how value moves
- Answers the strongest objection (a billionth of anything is worthless) in the open
- Publishes what nobody has solved, including the legal problem that could end it
- Shows the ladder from one member to a billion, with the test each rung must pass
- Says which rung it is actually on — rung one, not started
- Collects builders, architects, and **sceptics**, with disagreement as a first-class path

## Deliberately does NOT
- Claim to be an operating company — the schema says `Project`, and the FAQ leads with "no"
- Accept money, memberships or ventures — none of that exists yet
- Take sign-ups it cannot store: the endpoint refuses with a reason rather than dropping them
- Promise a timeline for anything above rung two
- Reuse the Foundernees visual language — a different argument gets a different voice
