# Foundernees — Scope

Bar to add a feature: **≥3 real target users blocked without it.** Otherwise → parking lot.
Second bar, specific to this product: **would it still work for someone on a cheap Android
over patchy data, reading at grade 8?** If not, it isn't in scope — it is a different product.

## Build 1 — the marketing site

### Does
- States the mission in language a first-time builder understands — not a pitch deck in HTML
- Explains the four stages (Learner → Builder → Foundernee → Founder) and that movement is earned, not scheduled
- Explains what a Foundership is — a grant paid in tools and credits, not cash, and why
- Shows the Year One clock: 14 Aug 2026 → 14 Aug 2027, days remaining, live
- Shows the five numbers publicly, including when they are zero
- Captures a builder: name, contact, city, and one line on what they want to build
- Attributes every signup to a source so distribution can be measured, not guessed
- Loads fast enough to be usable on a mid-tier Android over slow 4G

### Deliberately does NOT
- Log anyone in — no accounts, no dashboard, no sessions
- Teach anything — no lessons, no videos, no curriculum
- Take money — no payments, no donations, no Stripe
- Run a community — no forum, no chat, no comments
- Accept Stage Three or Stage Four applications — that gate opens on the platform
- Host a blog on day one — the manifesto is the content; a blog is earned, not assumed
- Ship an admin panel beyond a read-only list of signups

## Build 2 — the platform (scope drafted, not committed)

### Does (intended)
- Stage One: an account, and a first session that ends with the person having made one thing
- Stage Two: a place to ship, show, and be seen shipping
- The progression itself: earned movement between stages, assessed on artifacts

### Deliberately does NOT (intended)
- Become a course platform — the thing that just got cheap is exactly what not to sell
- Gate Stage One behind payment, invitation, or English fluency
- Replace the tools (Claude, Lovable, Replit) — it points at them and removes the friction

## The standing constraint

The people this is built for — the office boys, the ones everyone wrote off — are the
design target, not a later accessibility pass. Any decision that trades their access for
polish loses. That constraint is enforced numerically by M1 and M2 in `goal.md` so it
cannot quietly become a slogan.
