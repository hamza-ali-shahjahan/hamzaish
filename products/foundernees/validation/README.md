### 2026-08-16 — Building the marketing site before validation

- **Why now:** The site *is* the validation instrument. Year One's clock started on
  14 Aug 2026 and there is nothing to point anyone at — every conversation with a
  target user right now costs a link that does not exist. A marketing site with a
  measured signup form is cheap (days), fully reversible (no data model to migrate,
  no users to strand), and produces the exact evidence the 5 conversations would:
  do Pakistani first-time builders want this enough to give a name and a city.
- **What would make me stop and validate:** the **platform** build. Stage One and
  Stage Two are expensive and hard to reverse — a wrong first-session design costs
  months. The 5 conversations happen before a line of platform code.
- **Catch-up trigger:** whichever comes first —
  1. before Build 2 (the platform) starts, or
  2. at 200 real signups, interview 5 of them from the list the site produced.

  The second is the better version: the site hands you a sampling frame instead of
  making you find one.
