# 0001 — Year One scoreboard: 100K is the shipping number, as written

- **Date**: 2026-08-16
- **Status**: **CONFIRMED by the operator**, 2026-08-16.
- **Decision**: Year One (14 Aug 2026 → 14 Aug 2027) is set at **10,000,000 reached ·
  100,000 builders who ship a first thing · 10,000 completing the programme · 1,000
  Founderships · 200 companies alive at the next 14 August**, with **1,000,000 builders
  onboarded** as the operating input. "The first 100K builders in Pakistan" keeps its
  plain meaning — 100K who *ship*, not 100K who *sign up*.
- **Why**: The operator was shown the arithmetic and chose the ambitious read
  deliberately. The reasoning that carried it: a movement whose founding number is
  quietly deflated in its first week is not a movement. The brief already says the team
  doesn't have a ten-year plan and isn't sure how to do most of what lies ahead — a
  target sized to what feels safe would contradict the document it came from.
- **What this commits to**: a clean decimal cascade — 10M → 1M → 100K → 10K → 1K → 200.
  Each step is a 10% conversion (20% survival on the last). The 10% reached→onboarded
  step is achievable **only** if most reach is partner-referred rather than cold social
  traffic. That is the load-bearing assumption of the entire year.
- **The consequence, stated so it cannot be forgotten**: **the product is not the risk —
  the signature is.** Ten million reached in Pakistan in 362 days has precedent
  (DigiSkills.pk cleared millions of enrolments) but every precedent ran on state or
  telco distribution. Foundernees can build a flawless site and a flawless Stage One and
  still miss #1 by an order of magnitude if no national channel opens. Partnerships are
  therefore a gated workstream — P1 (20 named targets, due 14 Sep 2026), P2 (first
  signature covering ≥500K people, due 14 Nov 2026), P3 (2.5M reached, due 14 Feb 2027)
  — not a marketing afterthought.
- **What it changed in the build**: the v1 signup form gains a **role** field
  (builder / volunteer / partner-institution) so the partner pipeline fills from the
  first visitor. Cost: near zero. It replaces the separate partner page that was scoped
  out, keeping the site at five pages.
- **What would prove it wrong**: P2 missing on 14 Nov 2026 — no partner committed to
  ≥500K people at the quarter mark. That is the moment to revise #1 **in public**, not on
  13 Aug 2027. Equally disconfirming in the other direction: a measured ship-rate above
  20% in the first cohort, which would say the funnel was pessimistic and the targets
  should rise.
- **Revisit trigger**: 14 Nov 2026 (P2, mandatory) · 14 Feb 2027 (P3, mandatory) · or
  immediately on the first signed national distribution partnership.
- **Alternatives considered**: (superseded) an earlier recommendation in this file proposed 1.5M reached / 20K
  shippers / 100K onboarded. The operator reviewed the funnel arithmetic and chose the
  targets above instead. Recorded rather than deleted, so the trade-off stays visible if
  the numbers are ever revisited.
